Study Aid Disclaimer

This app is an independent study aid, not an official government product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by PHMSA, the U.S. DOT, FMCSA, TSA, or any state DMV. Passing practice or mock exams does not award or imply a HazMat (H) endorsement — always confirm requirements with your state DMV and complete the TSA background check. First-time applicants must also complete ELDT theory training separately.

The Real Exam

The H endorsement knowledge test is 30 questions with an 80% passing score, based on the FMCSA CDL Manual Section 9. The TSA security threat assessment fee is $85.25, renewed every 5 years.
